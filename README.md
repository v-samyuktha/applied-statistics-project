# Screen Time and Sleep Habits — Applied Statistics

Team project for **MA4240: Applied Statistics** at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH).

## Overview

A survey-based statistical study exploring the relationships between screen time and reported sleep duration, sleep quality and bedtime. The analysis uses 244 student responses collected through a Google Form.

## Methods

- Exploratory data analysis and descriptive statistics
- Data visualisation of screen-time patterns and sleep habits
- Hypothesis testing
- Spearman rank correlation analysis

## Limitations

The study uses a volunteer sample of students from IITH and self-reported survey responses. The findings are exploratory, may not generalise to other populations and should not be interpreted as establishing causation.
